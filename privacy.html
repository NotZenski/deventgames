Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Devent Inc. ("Devent Games", "we", "us") handles information when you visit deventgames.com (the "Site"). Our games run on Roblox, and gameplay data there is governed by Roblox's own policies (see "Our games on Roblox" below).

Information we collect

Information you give us. If you use our contact form, we receive your email address, your message, and your confirmation that you are over 13.

Information collected automatically. The Site does not use cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics. Like any website, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and font provider (Google Fonts) may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your browser loads the Site.

How we use information

  • To read and reply to messages you send us.
  • To keep the Site secure and working properly.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Site:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the Site.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the Site's typeface.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users.

Our games on Roblox

Our games are played on the Roblox platform. Roblox collects and processes player data under the Roblox Privacy Policy. Within our games we only access the information Roblox makes available to developers, such as usernames, user IDs, and in-game progress, and we use it solely to operate the games.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and our contact form requires you to confirm that you are over 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the Site. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep information

We keep contact form messages only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record keeping, then delete them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you through our contact form. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through our contact form.
